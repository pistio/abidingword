## Common questions

<details>
<summary><strong>Do I need the internet?</strong></summary>

No. Abiding Word works completely offline. The internet is only used, now and
then, to look for a newer version. If there is no connection, nothing changes.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Is my data sent anywhere?</strong></summary>

No. There is no account and no cloud. Your studies, notes and library stay in
one folder on your computer (see [Backups](08-settings-updates-backups.md#backups)).

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Does Abiding Word use artificial intelligence?</strong></summary>

**Only if you want it, and only when you prepare to teach.** Everything else
in Abiding Word can be traced to something real: a verse in a Bible, a page or
heading in a book, something you wrote. When it says a book "mentions John
15:5", the book really contains that reference.

The **assistant** is optional and **off** until you turn it on in Settings. It
helps with *your* writing in a teaching (see [Preparing to teach](10-teaching.md)):

- it acts only when you ask, and says first what it will see and where it goes;
- it suggests, comments and checks; it never writes your sermon or lesson, and
  never changes your text unless you choose a suggestion (and you can undo it);
- it keeps your voice instead of making your writing sound like someone else's;
- it shows what its comments are based on, marks invented examples as
  invented, and offers real stories only with the sources a search found;
- it can run on your own computer (Ollama, LM Studio), so nothing leaves it.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Which Bibles can I use?</strong></summary>

Nine free Bibles come with Abiding Word: the **Berean Standard Bible** and
the **World English Bible** (English); the **Lutherbibel 1912**, the
**Schlachter-Bibel 1951** and the unrevised **Elberfelder 1905** (Deutsch);
**성경전서 개역한글판** (한국어); **Louis Segond 1910** (Français);
**Reina-Valera 1909** (Español); and the **Bíblia Livre** (Português). See [Add a Bible](01-getting-started.md#add-a-bible).

You can also add any other Bible you have as a file in the common **USFM**
format (often a `.zip` download from a Bible publisher or from eBible.org).
Abiding Word checks it carefully before adding it.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>I switched translations. Where are my highlights?</strong></summary>

They are still there. Marks belong to the verse, not to one translation. A
highlight on a few words may show *"Not shown in this wording"* if the other
translation uses different words. It is not lost.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Why can't I click Bible references in a PDF's Original view?</strong></summary>

For a PDF, the Original view shows the real printed pages. Making those pages
clickable needs exact positions for every word, and that is planned for a
coming version. Until then, use the **Reading** view, where every reference is
clickable.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>My PDF can't be added. It says it is scanned.</strong></summary>

Some PDFs are just photos of pages, with no real text inside. Abiding Word
can't read those yet. Reading scanned pages is planned for the future.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>A German book says "mal sehen". Why is "mal" not a link to Malachi?</strong></summary>

On purpose. "mal" is an ordinary German word. Abiding Word only treats *Mal* as
Malachi when it has a capital M **and** a verse, like *Mal 4,3*.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>I moved the panes around and want the usual layout back.</strong></summary>

**Settings → Workspace layout → Reset layout.** Your work is not affected.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>How do I make a backup?</strong></summary>

Close Abiding Word and copy the **Abiding Word** folder (shown in Settings)
somewhere safe. See [Backups](08-settings-updates-backups.md#backups).

</details>

## Word list

| Word | Meaning in Abiding Word |
|---|---|
| **Pane** | One of the three side-by-side areas: Scripture, Study, Sources |
| **Passage** | A part of the Bible, such as *John 15:1–11* |
| **Translation** | One version of the Bible, such as the World English Bible (WEB) |
| **Marks** | Your highlights, notes and bookmarks on Bible verses |
| **Study** | Your work on one passage, word or topic, written in sections |
| **Project** | A group of studies that belong together, such as a sermon series |
| **Template** | The ready-made list of sections a new study starts with |
| **Study note** | A short entry in a study with a kind: observation, question, application… |
| **Library** | Your own books, articles and notes, stored inside Abiding Word |
| **Source** | One item in your library |
| **Reading / Original** | Two ways to view a source: clean text, or the real pages |
| **Reference** | A written pointer to a passage, such as *Rom 8:1* or *Joh 15,5* |
| **Concordance** | A list of every place a word appears in a Bible |
| **USFM** | A common file format for Bibles |
| **Test version** | An early version for trying out; it updates itself |
