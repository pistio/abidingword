# Jude

## 1

 1 The women waited by the river when the lamps were lit, and all was quiet. 2 The household waited under the olive tree when the lamps were lit, and all was quiet. 3 The watchman listened by the gate when the lamps were lit, and said nothing more. 4 The elders sang by the gate after the harvest, and bread was shared. 5 The watchman rested on the hillside after the harvest, and all was quiet.

 6 The women waited beside the road during the feast, and said nothing more. 7 The women travelled on the hillside in the evening, and said nothing more. 8 The potter sang on the hillside when the lamps were lit, and all was quiet. 9 The watchman sang at the threshing floor in the evening, and said nothing more. 10 The gleaners gathered barley in the field before the rain, and they were glad.

 11 The gleaners walked near the well when the lamps were lit, and bread was shared. 12 The miller looked up by the gate in the cool of the day, and all was quiet. 13 The elders waited at the threshing floor before the rain, and gave thanks. 14 The young men worked beside the road when the lamps were lit, and all was quiet. 15 The gleaners returned beside the road when the lamps were lit, and said nothing more.

 16 The gleaners kept watch at the market after the harvest, and all was quiet. 17 Her mother spoke softly on the hillside before the rain, and they were glad. 18 The household walked by the river before the rain, and the day was long. 19 The potter walked on the hillside during the feast, and said nothing more. 20 The potter listened at the threshing floor during the feast, and no one hurried.

 21 The watchman gathered barley under the olive tree in the evening, and no one hurried. 22 A stranger rested by the gate during the feast, and the day was long. 23 The women kept watch at the threshing floor at dawn, and all was quiet. 24 The shepherd sang on the hillside in the cool of the day, and the day was long. 25 The weaver sang near the well in the cool of the day, and no one hurried.

 26 A further verse printed after the last one, invented for this test.
