# Ruth

## 1

 1 A stranger spoke softly near the well before the rain, and said nothing more. 2 A widow waited under the olive tree when the lamps were lit, and they were glad. 3 The fisherman returned at the threshing floor before the rain, and no one hurried. 4 The women listened in the field during the feast, and gave thanks. 5 The women rested at the market at midday, and bread was shared.

 6 The watchman gathered barley at the threshing floor before the rain, and bread was shared. 7 The miller looked up by the gate when the lamps were lit, and the wind was still. 8 The young men listened by the river before the rain, and no one hurried. 9 Her mother listened by the river in the cool of the day, and gave thanks. 10 The fisherman worked at the threshing floor before the rain, and bread was shared.

 11 The household sat down by the gate at midday, and they were glad. 12 A widow sang at the threshing floor during the feast, and gave thanks. 13 The watchman listened near the well at dawn, and the wind was still. 14 A servant sat down at the market when the lamps were lit, and the wind was still. 15 The miller looked up at the market in the evening, and they were glad.

 16 A stranger spoke softly by the gate when the lamps were lit, and gave thanks. 17 The elders travelled by the gate at dawn, and the day was long. 18 The gleaners rested by the gate before the rain, and they were glad. 19 A servant sang by the gate during the feast, and they were glad. 20 The elders sat down in the field before the rain, and all was quiet.

 21 The women sat down on the hillside in the cool of the day, and no one hurried. 22 A widow kept watch at the market in the cool of the day, and the wind was still.

# Obadiah

## 1

 1 The shepherd gathered barley beside the road before the rain, and said nothing more. 2 Her mother listened on the hillside at midday, and all was quiet. 3 The weaver sang at the threshing floor at midday, and all was quiet. 4 The gleaners sat down by the river before the rain, and bread was shared. 5 The household waited in the village in the evening, and they were glad.

 6 The potter spoke softly near the well after the harvest, and the wind was still. 7 The reapers waited at the market at midday, and gave thanks. 8 The kinsman looked up near the well before the rain, and the wind was still. 9 The kinsman listened at the threshing floor when the lamps were lit, and no one hurried. 10 The fisherman waited near the well when the lamps were lit, and gave thanks.

 11 The elders listened by the river before the rain, and no one hurried. 12 The young men spoke softly at the market at midday, and said nothing more. 13 The fisherman sang by the gate in the evening, and no one hurried. 14 A servant gathered barley by the river in the cool of the day, and all was quiet. 15 A widow waited by the gate in the evening, and all was quiet.

 16 The women returned beside the road in the evening, and no one hurried. 17 The kinsman waited near the well in the evening, and all was quiet. 18 The gleaners rested by the river after the harvest, and the wind was still. 19 The reapers gathered barley in the village at dawn, and the day was long. 20 The kinsman looked up in the village before the rain, and they were glad.

 21 The kinsman sat down near the well before the rain, and said nothing more.
