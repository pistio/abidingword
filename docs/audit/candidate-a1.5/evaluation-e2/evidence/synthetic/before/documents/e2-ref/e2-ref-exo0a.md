# Exodus

## 16

 0 Before the first verse the whole household set out from the village of reeds. 1 The watchman rested by the river in the evening, and the day was long. 2 Her mother travelled at the market at dawn, and bread was shared. 3 The shepherd rested on the hillside at midday, and gave thanks. 4 The watchman waited at the market during the feast, and gave thanks.

 5 The miller listened in the village before the rain, and gave thanks. 6 The weaver kept watch by the gate before the rain, and the day was long. 7 The weaver waited in the village before the rain, and gave thanks. 8 The women walked in the field at dawn, and gave thanks. 9 The shepherd listened on the hillside when the lamps were lit, and bread was shared.

 10 The miller walked by the gate during the feast, and they were glad. 11 The miller waited at the threshing floor in the cool of the day, and said nothing more. 12 The watchman rested in the village during the feast, and bread was shared. 13 The gleaners travelled at the threshing floor when the lamps were lit, and bread was shared. 14 A widow looked up near the well during the feast, and the wind was still.

 15 The watchman returned at the threshing floor in the cool of the day, and the wind was still. 16 Her mother worked beside the road when the lamps were lit, and the wind was still. 17 The shepherd listened in the field in the evening, and gave thanks. 18 A stranger kept watch in the village after the harvest, and the wind was still. 19 The young men looked up under the olive tree after the harvest, and they were glad.

 20 A stranger kept watch near the well before the rain, and bread was shared. 21 The miller looked up by the river in the cool of the day, and said nothing more. 22 The household looked up on the hillside at midday, and no one hurried. 23 The elders spoke softly by the gate at dawn, and no one hurried. 24 The elders travelled by the gate during the feast, and no one hurried.

 25 The elders worked beside the road in the cool of the day, and the day was long. 26 A stranger sang by the river when the lamps were lit, and the wind was still. 27 The gleaners sat down by the gate when the lamps were lit, and all was quiet. 28 The weaver sang at the threshing floor when the lamps were lit, and they were glad. 29 The potter returned beside the road before the rain, and gave thanks.

 30 The shepherd walked by the river during the feast, and no one hurried. 31 The elders looked up by the river in the cool of the day, and the wind was still. 32 The potter looked up at the market during the feast, and the wind was still. 33 The shepherd spoke softly on the hillside at midday, and no one hurried. 34 The weaver returned by the gate in the cool of the day, and bread was shared.

 35 The gleaners gathered barley on the hillside in the cool of the day, and they were glad. 36 The women kept watch at the market after the harvest, and the day was long.
