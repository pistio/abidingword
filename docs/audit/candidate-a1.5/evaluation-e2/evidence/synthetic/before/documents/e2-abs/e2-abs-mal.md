# Malachi

## 1

 1 The shepherd sat down at the market after the harvest, and the day was long. 2 A widow worked near the well in the evening, and the wind was still. 3 The shepherd listened in the village during the feast, and gave thanks. 4 The women kept watch on the hillside after the harvest, and said nothing more. 5 The elders looked up at the market before the rain, and bread was shared.

 6 The shepherd waited at the market when the lamps were lit, and all was quiet. 7 The potter gathered barley in the village after the harvest, and gave thanks. 8 The reapers kept watch at the market in the evening, and the day was long. 9 The gleaners gathered barley at the threshing floor in the evening, and the day was long. 10 The household waited under the olive tree before the rain, and no one hurried.

 11 The reapers sang by the gate before the rain, and all was quiet. 12 The potter gathered barley under the olive tree before the rain, and said nothing more. 13 The kinsman gathered barley under the olive tree during the feast, and bread was shared. 14 The household gathered barley at the threshing floor at dawn, and the wind was still.

## 2

 1 The weaver sat down beside the road in the cool of the day, and no one hurried. 2 The young men spoke softly at the threshing floor in the evening, and said nothing more. 3 The watchman waited near the well at dawn, and all was quiet. 4 The potter listened by the gate before the rain, and said nothing more. 5 The fisherman returned in the field during the feast, and gave thanks.

 6 The elders sang by the gate in the evening, and the day was long. 7 The kinsman looked up under the olive tree during the feast, and said nothing more. 8 The women looked up beside the road at dawn, and said nothing more. 9 The miller returned at the threshing floor in the cool of the day, and all was quiet. 10 The fisherman sat down beside the road after the harvest, and they were glad.

 11 The fisherman travelled in the village after the harvest, and bread was shared. 12 The shepherd travelled under the olive tree during the feast, and bread was shared. 13 A servant returned by the river after the harvest, and bread was shared. 14 The household looked up at the threshing floor at midday, and said nothing more. 15 The gleaners travelled by the river during the feast, and the day was long.

 16 The shepherd waited at the market after the harvest, and the day was long. 17 A servant kept watch beside the road during the feast, and the day was long.

## 3

 1 A widow returned at the threshing floor at midday, and the wind was still. 2 The miller kept watch at the threshing floor in the cool of the day, and all was quiet. 3 The potter gathered barley at the market in the cool of the day, and they were glad. 4 The elders worked in the village after the harvest, and all was quiet. 5 The watchman rested near the well when the lamps were lit, and all was quiet.

 6 The fisherman gathered barley at the threshing floor when the lamps were lit, and they were glad. 7 The shepherd walked at the market when the lamps were lit, and bread was shared. 8 The women gathered barley in the village during the feast, and said nothing more. 9 The watchman returned under the olive tree before the rain, and gave thanks. 10 A stranger sat down on the hillside after the harvest, and gave thanks.

 11 A stranger walked in the field at midday, and the day was long. 12 The potter rested in the village after the harvest, and they were glad. 13 A stranger listened on the hillside after the harvest, and the wind was still. 14 The young men worked beside the road in the evening, and gave thanks. 15 The kinsman worked beside the road in the evening, and bread was shared.

 16 The watchman listened by the river when the lamps were lit, and gave thanks. 17 The kinsman looked up at the market during the feast, and said nothing more. 18 The elders listened at the threshing floor in the cool of the day, and said nothing more.

## 4

 6 The gleaners waited by the gate after the harvest, and bread was shared.
