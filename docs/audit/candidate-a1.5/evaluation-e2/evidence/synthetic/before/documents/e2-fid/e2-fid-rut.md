# Ruth

## 1

 1 Its the elders' doing, said the widow by the gate. 2 Hell come again at dawn, said the stranger in the field. 3 Were the reapers of his field, said the young men. 4 Truly I tell you today, you will rest in the village with me. 5 You are the kinsman who keeps the field?

 6 And she shall be set a part for the harvest of the barley. 7 Her keeper is nowhere in the field among the reapers. 8 The spirit of the LORD rested on the reapers at midday. 9 The Lord is my keeper in the evening and in the morning. 10 The women wept at the gate when the lamps were lit.

 11 A servant not the master spoke first at the market. 12 The watchman said, "Open the gate at dawn." 13 The watchman listened near the well at dawn, and the wind was still. 14 A servant sat down at the market when the lamps were lit, and the wind was still. 15 The miller looked up at the market in the evening, and they were glad.

 16 A stranger spoke softly by the gate when the lamps were lit, and gave thanks. 17 The elders travelled by the gate at dawn, and the day was long. 18 The gleaners rested by the gate before the rain, and they were glad. 19 A servant sang by the gate during the feast, and they were glad. 20 The elders sat down in the field before the rain, and all was quiet.

 21 The women sat down on the hillside in the cool of the day, and no one hurried. 22 A widow kept watch at the market in the cool of the day, and the wind was still.

## 2

 1 The miller worked under the olive tree in the cool of the day, and no one hurried. 2 The women waited at the threshing floor in the evening, and said nothing more. 3 The miller walked in the field after the harvest, and said nothing more. 4 The young men sat down at the threshing floor after the harvest, and the wind was still. 5 The elders sat down at the market in the evening, and they were glad.

 6 Her mother sat down at the market at midday, and no one hurried. 7 The miller looked up in the field when the lamps were lit, and all was quiet. 8 The reapers spoke softly under the olive tree in the evening, and gave thanks. 9 The potter worked in the village during the feast, and they were glad. 10 The elders kept watch in the village before the rain, and said nothing more.

 11 The reapers worked beside the road after the harvest, and all was quiet. 12 A stranger kept watch by the river when the lamps were lit, and the day was long. 13 The watchman kept watch in the field at dawn, and the wind was still. 14 A widow returned at the threshing floor before the rain, and all was quiet. 15 The kinsman spoke softly by the river at dawn, and said nothing more.

 16 The weaver returned at the threshing floor after the harvest, and all was quiet. 17 The watchman listened in the village after the harvest, and said nothing more. 18 The elders listened by the gate before the rain, and all was quiet. 19 A widow spoke softly at the market after the harvest, and the wind was still. 20 The women sat down on the hillside during the feast, and bread was shared.

 21 The miller waited by the river in the cool of the day, and said nothing more. 22 The shepherd worked in the field at dawn, and the wind was still. 23 Her mother gathered barley on the hillside when the lamps were lit, and the day was long.

 11 A stranger waited near the well at midday, and gave thanks. 12 A stranger spoke softly beside the road at dawn, and the wind was still. 13 The fisherman travelled near the well before the rain, and no one hurried. 14 A stranger sat down in the village after the harvest, and bread was shared. 15 A widow returned near the well after the harvest, and no one hurried.

 16 The women waited in the field at midday, and bread was shared. 17 The shepherd worked by the river at dawn, and bread was shared. 18 The potter gathered barley by the river at dawn, and said nothing more.
