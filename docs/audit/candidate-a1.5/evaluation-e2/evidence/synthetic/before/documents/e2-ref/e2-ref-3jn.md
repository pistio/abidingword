# 3 John

## 1

 1 The women walked by the river when the lamps were lit, and they were glad. 2 The women sat down beside the road in the cool of the day, and all was quiet. 3 The weaver sat down near the well in the cool of the day, and gave thanks. 4 Her mother rested beside the road before the rain, and the wind was still. 5 The women walked near the well after the harvest, and gave thanks.

 6 The gleaners returned near the well in the evening, and no one hurried. 7 The reapers sang in the village after the harvest, and bread was shared. 8 The young men kept watch in the village at dawn, and said nothing more. 9 The gleaners spoke softly beside the road at dawn, and they were glad. 10 The household travelled near the well at dawn, and gave thanks.

 11 A servant spoke softly on the hillside before the rain, and gave thanks. 12a The women looked up near the well in the evening, and the wind was still. 13 The watchman waited under the olive tree when the lamps were lit, and bread was shared. 14 The young men looked up beside the road during the feast, and bread was shared. 15 The shepherd travelled near the well before the rain, and gave thanks.

## 2

 1 The watchman returned near the well before the rain, and gave thanks. 2 Her mother sang in the village in the evening, and no one hurried. 3 The gleaners listened at the market during the feast, and the day was long. 4 The gleaners waited by the river in the evening, and said nothing more.
