# Joel

## 1

 1 The young men kept watch near the well after the harvest, and the wind was still. 2 The fisherman sang by the river during the feast, and the day was long. 3 A stranger gathered barley near the well in the evening, and the wind was still. 4 The fisherman returned on the hillside at midday, and bread was shared. 5 The shepherd returned beside the road at dawn, and they were glad.

 6 The kinsman kept watch in the village at midday, and the day was long. 7 The shepherd travelled by the river in the cool of the day, and bread was shared. 8 The household worked near the well in the cool of the day, and gave thanks. 9 The household sat down on the hillside when the lamps were lit, and said nothing more. 10 The women gathered barley in the village in the evening, and bread was shared.

 11 The household spoke softly at the threshing floor at dawn, and gave thanks. 12 The watchman gathered barley by the gate when the lamps were lit, and they were glad. 13 A stranger returned in the village during the feast, and all was quiet. 14 The elders worked near the well in the evening, and no one hurried. 15 The shepherd rested under the olive tree in the cool of the day, and all was quiet.

 16 The potter sat down by the river at midday, and gave thanks. 17 The household worked beside the road at dawn, and the wind was still. 18 A widow spoke softly under the olive tree before the rain, and no one hurried. 19 The shepherd kept watch in the field during the feast, and no one hurried. 20 The elders travelled by the river when the lamps were lit, and they were glad.
