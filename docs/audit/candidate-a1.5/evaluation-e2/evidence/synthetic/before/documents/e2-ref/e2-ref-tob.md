# Obadiah

## 1

 1 The shepherd gathered barley beside the road before the rain, and said nothing more. 2 Her mother listened on the hillside at midday, and all was quiet. 3 The weaver sang at the threshing floor at midday, and all was quiet. 4 The gleaners sat down by the river before the rain, and bread was shared. 5 The household waited in the village in the evening, and they were glad.

 6 The potter spoke softly near the well after the harvest, and the wind was still. 7 The reapers waited at the market at midday, and gave thanks. 8 The kinsman looked up near the well before the rain, and the wind was still. 9 The kinsman listened at the threshing floor when the lamps were lit, and no one hurried. 10 The fisherman waited near the well when the lamps were lit, and gave thanks.

 11 The elders listened by the river before the rain, and no one hurried. 12 The young men spoke softly at the market at midday, and said nothing more. 13 The fisherman sang by the gate in the evening, and no one hurried. 14 A servant gathered barley by the river in the cool of the day, and all was quiet. 15 A widow waited by the gate in the evening, and all was quiet.

 16 The women returned beside the road in the evening, and no one hurried. 17 The kinsman waited near the well in the evening, and all was quiet. 18 The gleaners rested by the river after the harvest, and the wind was still. 19 The reapers gathered barley in the village at dawn, and the day was long. 20 The kinsman looked up in the village before the rain, and they were glad.

 21 The kinsman sat down near the well before the rain, and said nothing more.

# Tobit

## 1

 1 The potter travelled in the village before the rain, and all was quiet. 2 The household spoke softly in the field before the rain, and said nothing more. 3 The women returned at the market at midday, and the day was long. 4 The young men rested at the threshing floor when the lamps were lit, and the wind was still. 5 The weaver waited by the river in the evening, and the day was long.

 6 The shepherd spoke softly in the village in the evening, and the wind was still. 7 The weaver spoke softly at the market at midday, and gave thanks. 8 The kinsman walked at the market when the lamps were lit, and said nothing more. 9 The household listened by the gate at midday, and the day was long. 10 The shepherd sang near the well in the evening, and the day was long.

 11 The young men worked near the well in the cool of the day, and the day was long. 12 The miller spoke softly near the well in the cool of the day, and no one hurried. 13 The miller sat down at the threshing floor before the rain, and gave thanks. 14 A servant listened at the threshing floor in the cool of the day, and the day was long. 15 The reapers walked at the threshing floor in the cool of the day, and all was quiet.

 16 The elders worked by the gate after the harvest, and said nothing more. 17 The gleaners listened on the hillside at dawn, and they were glad. 18 A widow kept watch beside the road at dawn, and bread was shared. 19 The young men sat down at the threshing floor when the lamps were lit, and the day was long. 20 The elders spoke softly by the gate in the evening, and the wind was still.

 21 The household travelled in the village in the evening, and the day was long. 22 The household spoke softly by the gate in the evening, and bread was shared.
