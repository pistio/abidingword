# Jude

## 1

 1 A widow returned on the hillside in the evening, and they were glad. 2 A servant sang by the river at dawn, and all was quiet. 3 The fisherman returned by the gate after the harvest, and the day was long. 4-5 The reapers spoke softly near the well before the rain, and all was quiet. A stranger returned at the market at dawn, and all was quiet. 6 The watchman looked up at the market during the feast, and the day was long.

 7 The fisherman walked at the market before the rain, and the wind was still. 8 A servant sang beside the road during the feast, and the day was long. 10 The watchman returned at the threshing floor at midday, and no one hurried. 11 The watchman spoke softly in the field at dawn, and no one hurried. 12 —

 13 The potter listened on the hillside during the feast, and all was quiet. 14 [Omitted in the earliest copies] 15 The gleaners listened beside the road when the lamps were lit, and they were glad. 16 A widow sat down on the hillside during the feast, and they were glad. 17 The women walked at the market at dawn, and all was quiet.

 18 A servant sang by the gate at dawn, and no one hurried. 19 The weaver worked at the threshing floor when the lamps were lit, and bread was shared. 21 The shepherd sang on the hillside when the lamps were lit, and the wind was still. 22 The women worked near the well during the feast, and all was quiet. 23 A stranger waited beside the road after the harvest, and the wind was still.

 24 The weaver worked by the gate before the rain, and the wind was still. 25 The fisherman returned in the village when the lamps were lit, and the wind was still.
