# Malachi

## 1

 1 A stranger rested at the market in the cool of the day, and gave thanks. 2 The reapers waited on the hillside at midday, and bread was shared. 3 The fisherman kept watch beside the road when the lamps were lit, and no one hurried. 4 The gleaners gathered barley at the market after the harvest, and the wind was still. 5 The household travelled under the olive tree during the feast, and all was quiet.

 6 The reapers listened at the threshing floor in the cool of the day, and no one hurried. 7 The gleaners travelled at the threshing floor at dawn, and all was quiet. 8 A servant sang at the market at midday, and gave thanks. 9 The young men sat down near the well at dawn, and the wind was still. 10 The gleaners kept watch under the olive tree at dawn, and they were glad.

 11 A widow looked up by the gate before the rain, and bread was shared. 12 Her mother waited by the river before the rain, and gave thanks. 13 The household sat down in the village after the harvest, and they were glad. 14 The weaver sat down at the market when the lamps were lit, and all was quiet.

## 2

 1 The young men returned in the village at dawn, and they were glad. 2 The watchman sat down on the hillside after the harvest, and gave thanks. 3 The gleaners worked in the village after the harvest, and said nothing more. 4 The reapers gathered barley by the river at midday, and they were glad. 5 The shepherd looked up in the field at dawn, and said nothing more.

 6 The miller sang under the olive tree after the harvest, and they were glad. 7 The potter kept watch under the olive tree when the lamps were lit, and said nothing more. 8 Her mother spoke softly near the well at midday, and gave thanks. 9 The household rested by the gate in the cool of the day, and the wind was still. 10 Her mother kept watch at the market at midday, and the wind was still.

 11 The elders travelled in the village at midday, and said nothing more. 12 The shepherd rested by the river in the evening, and the wind was still. 13 The watchman kept watch in the field when the lamps were lit, and the wind was still. 14 The women gathered barley beside the road during the feast, and bread was shared. 15 The shepherd sang in the village before the rain, and bread was shared.

 16 The weaver walked by the gate in the cool of the day, and gave thanks. 17 The women looked up at the threshing floor in the cool of the day, and they were glad.

## 3

 1 The weaver looked up on the hillside at dawn, and said nothing more. 2 The weaver gathered barley by the gate when the lamps were lit, and said nothing more. 3 A widow rested in the field when the lamps were lit, and bread was shared. 4 The household rested in the village when the lamps were lit, and no one hurried. 5 The household travelled beside the road before the rain, and they were glad.

 6 A servant listened at the threshing floor at dawn, and gave thanks. 7 The elders spoke softly in the field during the feast, and the wind was still. 8 Her mother gathered barley at the threshing floor when the lamps were lit, and gave thanks. 9 A widow kept watch by the river at dawn, and said nothing more. 10 The potter spoke softly near the well in the evening, and the wind was still.

 11 The weaver sat down under the olive tree at midday, and gave thanks. 12 A stranger travelled at the threshing floor during the feast, and no one hurried. 13 The women listened by the gate at dawn, and no one hurried. 14 The elders sat down near the well in the cool of the day, and no one hurried. 15 Her mother travelled in the field in the cool of the day, and no one hurried.

 16 The potter kept watch at the threshing floor when the lamps were lit, and said nothing more. 17 A widow gathered barley by the gate when the lamps were lit, and no one hurried. 18 The kinsman kept watch beside the road when the lamps were lit, and they were glad.

## 4

 1 The elders rested beside the road in the cool of the day, and said nothing more. 2 The potter looked up under the olive tree after the harvest, and they were glad. 3 A widow walked beside the road in the cool of the day, and the day was long. 4 The watchman listened at the market before the rain, and they were glad. 5 The miller returned at the market at dawn, and gave thanks.

 6 The gleaners spoke softly in the village in the evening, and all was quiet.
