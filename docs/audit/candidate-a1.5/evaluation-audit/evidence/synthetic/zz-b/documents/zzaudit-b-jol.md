# Joel

## 1

 1 The kinsman gathered barley on the hillside before the rain, and no one hurried. 2 The reapers worked under the olive tree before the rain, and the wind was still. 3 The household rested by the gate at midday, and the wind was still. 4 The watchman kept watch by the river in the cool of the day, and all was quiet. 5 The watchman sang at the threshing floor before the rain, and no one hurried.

 6 The weaver sang in the field at dawn, and they were glad. 7 The gleaners kept watch under the olive tree before the rain, and the day was long. 8 The women listened in the field in the evening, and bread was shared. 9 A widow walked by the river before the rain, and the day was long. 10 The fisherman worked at the threshing floor at midday, and said nothing more.

 11 A widow sat down by the river at midday, and they were glad. 12 The household worked near the well in the cool of the day, and the wind was still. 13 The gleaners listened beside the road after the harvest, and the wind was still. 14 The fisherman rested near the well at dawn, and no one hurried. 15 The fisherman sat down by the gate in the cool of the day, and no one hurried.

 16 A stranger looked up beside the road at dawn, and the day was long. 17 The miller walked by the river before the rain, and gave thanks. 18 The fisherman sang at the threshing floor in the cool of the day, and gave thanks. 19 A servant sat down at the market in the evening, and bread was shared. 20 The watchman travelled near the well when the lamps were lit, and bread was shared.

 16 A servant listened under the olive tree at dawn, and all was quiet. 17 The reapers looked up beside the road in the cool of the day, and the wind was still. 18 The weaver listened at the market in the evening, and gave thanks. 19 The miller gathered barley at the market when the lamps were lit, and the wind was still. 20 Her mother gathered barley beside the road during the feast, and the wind was still.

 21 The kinsman gathered barley at the market in the cool of the day, and the day was long. 22 The reapers spoke softly by the gate during the feast, and the day was long. 23 The weaver spoke softly near the well during the feast, and said nothing more. 24 A servant gathered barley near the well after the harvest, and gave thanks. 25 A servant worked by the gate when the lamps were lit, and the day was long.

 26 The shepherd rested at the market at midday, and the wind was still. 27 The shepherd sang under the olive tree after the harvest, and the day was long.
