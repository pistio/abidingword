# Exodus

## 16

 1 The women listened beside the road at dawn, and bread was shared. 2 The elders looked up on the hillside before the rain, and bread was shared. 3 The watchman worked near the well at midday, and the wind was still. 4 The watchman spoke softly by the gate during the feast, and they were glad. 5 The gleaners rested in the village in the cool of the day, and gave thanks.

 6 The watchman walked by the river after the harvest, and the day was long. 7 The watchman sat down at the threshing floor before the rain, and gave thanks. 8 The weaver spoke softly at the threshing floor after the harvest, and gave thanks. 9 The women gathered barley near the well when the lamps were lit, and no one hurried. 10 The weaver looked up on the hillside at dawn, and the wind was still.

 11 A widow gathered barley near the well at midday, and gave thanks. 12 The elders spoke softly at the threshing floor in the cool of the day, and they were glad. 13 The weaver rested at the market in the evening, and they were glad. 14 The fisherman sang at the market before the rain, and the day was long. 15 The kinsman waited beside the road before the rain, and all was quiet.

 16 The miller spoke softly beside the road in the cool of the day, and gave thanks. 17 The gleaners gathered barley at the threshing floor during the feast, and the day was long. 18 The weaver kept watch under the olive tree before the rain, and gave thanks. 19 The watchman sat down in the village at dawn, and they were glad. 20 The kinsman worked beside the road when the lamps were lit, and the wind was still.

 21 A stranger worked beside the road at midday, and gave thanks. 22 Her mother spoke softly in the village when the lamps were lit, and gave thanks. 23 Her mother gathered barley in the village in the evening, and gave thanks. 24 The reapers returned on the hillside at midday, and said nothing more. 25 The miller sat down by the gate during the feast, and gave thanks.

 26 The fisherman looked up on the hillside at midday, and they were glad. 27 The fisherman travelled in the field at midday, and said nothing more. 28 The watchman kept watch in the village in the cool of the day, and the wind was still. 29 The potter travelled by the river at midday, and all was quiet. 30 The household returned on the hillside during the feast, and gave thanks.

 31 The young men looked up in the field before the rain, and the wind was still. 32 The young men rested at the market at midday, and bread was shared. 33 The elders walked by the river in the evening, and said nothing more. 34 The gleaners travelled at the market at dawn, and the day was long. 35 The elders sang at the market when the lamps were lit, and said nothing more.

 36 The miller sang on the hillside when the lamps were lit, and the wind was still.
