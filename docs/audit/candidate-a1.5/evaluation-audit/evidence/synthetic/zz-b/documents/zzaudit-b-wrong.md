# Obadiah

## 1

 1 The kinsman worked at the threshing floor at dawn, and the day was long. 2 The kinsman looked up at the market in the evening, and gave thanks. 3 They waited four days by the river. 4 The fisherman sang beside the road in the evening, and the day was long. 5 Her mother rested in the village in the cool of the day, and they were glad.

 6 A servant travelled in the field at midday, and all was quiet. 7 A stranger waited by the gate before the rain, and they were glad. 8 The shepherd kept watch in the field in the cool of the day, and bread was shared. 9 The watchman rested near the well in the cool of the day, and they were glad. 10 The elders worked by the river during the feast, and gave thanks.

 11 The shepherd returned near the well at midday, and they were glad. 12 The miller spoke softly at the market after the harvest, and all was quiet. 13 A widow waited under the olive tree after the harvest, and all was quiet. 14 The miller sat down at the threshing floor in the cool of the day, and the wind was still. 15 A servant worked by the gate in the evening, and said nothing more.

 16 A stranger sang in the village at midday, and the wind was still. 17 The shepherd gathered barley in the field when the lamps were lit, and bread was shared. 18 A widow rested under the olive tree after the harvest, and no one hurried. 19 The fisherman rested in the field during the feast, and no one hurried. 20 The household listened beside the road at dawn, and said nothing more.

 21 The potter travelled beside the road after the harvest, and the day was long.
