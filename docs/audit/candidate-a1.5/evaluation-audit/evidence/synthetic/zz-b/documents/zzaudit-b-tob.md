# Obadiah

## 1

 1 The kinsman worked at the threshing floor at dawn, and the day was long. 2 The kinsman looked up at the market in the evening, and gave thanks. 3 The household looked up by the gate during the feast, and bread was shared. 4 The fisherman sang beside the road in the evening, and the day was long. 5 Her mother rested in the village in the cool of the day, and they were glad.

 6 A servant travelled in the field at midday, and all was quiet. 7 A stranger waited by the gate before the rain, and they were glad. 8 The shepherd kept watch in the field in the cool of the day, and bread was shared. 9 The watchman rested near the well in the cool of the day, and they were glad. 10 The elders worked by the river during the feast, and gave thanks.

 11 The shepherd returned near the well at midday, and they were glad. 12 The miller spoke softly at the market after the harvest, and all was quiet. 13 A widow waited under the olive tree after the harvest, and all was quiet. 14 The miller sat down at the threshing floor in the cool of the day, and the wind was still. 15 A servant worked by the gate in the evening, and said nothing more.

 16 A stranger sang in the village at midday, and the wind was still. 17 The shepherd gathered barley in the field when the lamps were lit, and bread was shared. 18 A widow rested under the olive tree after the harvest, and no one hurried. 19 The fisherman rested in the field during the feast, and no one hurried. 20 The household listened beside the road at dawn, and said nothing more.

 21 The potter travelled beside the road after the harvest, and the day was long.

# Tobit

## 1

 1 Her mother gathered barley in the field before the rain, and the wind was still. 2 The elders kept watch on the hillside before the rain, and all was quiet. 3 The weaver returned in the village in the evening, and the wind was still. 4 The gleaners sang at the market when the lamps were lit, and no one hurried. 5 The kinsman worked at the market after the harvest, and said nothing more.

 6 The weaver rested beside the road at midday, and they were glad. 7 The potter walked in the village after the harvest, and bread was shared. 8 The reapers walked in the field before the rain, and bread was shared. 9 The young men worked at the threshing floor before the rain, and the wind was still. 10 The watchman returned by the river during the feast, and the day was long.

 11 The gleaners waited on the hillside in the cool of the day, and no one hurried. 12 The young men travelled by the river in the cool of the day, and the wind was still. 13 The fisherman spoke softly in the village in the cool of the day, and said nothing more. 14 The household looked up in the village during the feast, and the wind was still. 15 Her mother listened in the village in the cool of the day, and said nothing more.

 16 The shepherd returned in the village at dawn, and the day was long. 17 A stranger worked at the market before the rain, and the wind was still. 18 The shepherd walked by the gate during the feast, and gave thanks. 19 The elders kept watch under the olive tree at midday, and they were glad. 20 The reapers gathered barley in the village before the rain, and they were glad.

 21 Her mother kept watch at the threshing floor during the feast, and the day was long. 22 A stranger returned in the village in the cool of the day, and they were glad.
