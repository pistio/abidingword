# Ruth

## 1

 1 The gleaners waited in the field at dawn, and bread was shared. 2 The fisherman sang under the olive tree at dawn, and they were glad. 3 Her mother listened by the gate after the harvest, and they were glad. 4 A widow sang near the well in the evening, and no one hurried. 5 The watchman worked by the river before the rain, and bread was shared.

 6 The kinsman listened on the hillside after the harvest, and bread was shared. 7 The potter returned in the village during the feast, and no one hurried. 8 The potter sat down by the river during the feast, and the wind was still. 9 The elders worked at the threshing floor when the lamps were lit, and gave thanks. 10 The young men looked up under the olive tree during the feast, and gave thanks.

 11 The reapers rested by the river before the rain, and bread was shared. 12 The watchman waited by the gate at dawn, and all was quiet. 13 The miller waited on the hillside at dawn, and they were glad. 14 The gleaners walked in the village in the cool of the day, and gave thanks. 15 Her mother waited under the olive tree at midday, and said nothing more.

 16 The miller sang under the olive tree after the harvest, and all was quiet. 17 The reapers listened beside the road in the evening, and no one hurried. 18 The miller waited on the hillside in the cool of the day, and no one hurried. 19 The young men sang in the village when the lamps were lit, and no one hurried. 20 The watchman walked on the hillside before the rain, and they were glad.

 21 The women listened by the gate at midday, and the wind was still. 22 The potter worked under the olive tree in the cool of the day, and the day was long.

## 2

 1 The elders waited near the well in the cool of the day, and they were glad. 2 The household rested on the hillside in the evening, and gave thanks. 3 A widow worked on the hillside after the harvest, and gave thanks. 4 The weaver spoke softly on the hillside in the cool of the day, and bread was shared. 5 A widow walked beside the road at dawn, and the wind was still.

 6 The potter sat down under the olive tree during the feast, and they were glad. 7 The watchman gathered barley beside the road during the feast, and gave thanks. 8 Her mother spoke softly by the river in the evening, and the wind was still. 9 A servant looked up under the olive tree at midday, and all was quiet. 10 A widow kept watch at the threshing floor when the lamps were lit, and no one hurried.

 11 The household walked at the market during the feast, and they were glad. 12 The shepherd rested at the market during the feast, and bread was shared. 13 The shepherd gathered barley by the river when the lamps were lit, and gave thanks. 14 The elders kept watch in the village at dawn, and no one hurried. 15 The household worked under the olive tree at midday, and gave thanks.

 16 The shepherd travelled by the gate in the evening, and gave thanks. 17 The potter gathered barley in the field in the cool of the day, and no one hurried. 18 The fisherman gathered barley beside the road when the lamps were lit, and bread was shared. 19 A widow spoke softly at the threshing floor during the feast, and bread was shared. 20 The elders listened on the hillside in the cool of the day, and the wind was still.

 21 The watchman gathered barley in the field in the evening, and said nothing more. 22 The elders sang in the village when the lamps were lit, and bread was shared. 23 The household looked up under the olive tree at midday, and all was quiet.
